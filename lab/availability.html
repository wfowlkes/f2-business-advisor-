Model architecture

1. Full-availability sales

Estimate a same-weekday sales scenario from historical in-stock days, excluding stockout-constrained periods from the demand proxy.

2. Inventory availability

Estimate stockout risk through historical recurrence only when repeated events support it; otherwise use a smoothed state-transition hazard model.

3. Conditional realized sales

Combine full-availability and constrained-sales scenarios. Show the envelope of possible outcomes; never describe it as a calibrated confidence interval.

4. Historical skill gate

Why forecasts remain on HOLD

Unknown replenishment timing and non-periodic supply shocks cannot be resolved from sales data alone. A credible next step is to obtain aggregate daily inventory availability, purchase-order arrival estimates, supplier lead-time reliability, and planned promotions. Any future plan is an assumption, never actual future data.

Even when the new candidate improves on one simulated type, it sometimes performs worse on other generator designs. Those failures are preserved in the test report; this application does not silently promote a universal model.
