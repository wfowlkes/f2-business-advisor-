Evidence-backed bridge routing

The controller monitors completed daily observations, requires sustained evidence to switch and blends a specialized candidate with the existing forecast. It retains the previous method when testing does not support replacing it.

Promotion fatigue

Recent refunds and promotion anomalies trigger a conservative 7-day revenue bridge. The original 30-day forecast stays unchanged.

Supply disruption

Historical stockouts adjust expected sales for observed stock availability over 30 days. The previous 7-day model remains unchanged.

Excessive switching

Six confirmed daily signals, 70-day minimum dwell, a limit of two switches per 90 days, and ten-day blending reduce churn.

Fail closed

Missing records, ambiguous evidence or unverified strategy benefit prevent automated changes to real merchant forecasts.
