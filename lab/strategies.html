What Learning Lab should do next

Keep the original champion

Where synthetic tests fail, suffer high bias, or offer insufficient improvement, preserve the simple method and flag the failure.

Detect actual regimes

Build a leakage-safe regime detector from past orders, refund ratios, sales changes and known stockouts. Do not use synthetic type labels in a real forecast.

Stress beyond our generator

Change supply lead times, shock timing, promotional calendars and seasonal patterns in a new independently designed simulator.

Demand external evidence

No approval of live Shopify recommendations without authorized, reconciled merchant history, prospective validation and error calibration.
